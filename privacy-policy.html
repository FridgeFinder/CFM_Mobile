Privacy Policy for FridgeFinder

Effective Date: December 2025

Last Updated: December 2025

Introduction

FridgeFinder ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Location Data

  • What we collect: Your device's precise GPS location
  • Why we collect it: To show nearby community fridges on the map and calculate distances
  • How it's used: Displayed on the map in real-time, used for distance calculations
  • Stored: Location is NOT stored on our servers. It is only used in real-time on your device and sent to our API to fetch nearby fridges. The location data is not retained after the API request completes.
  • Optional: You can use the app without granting location access, but map functionality and distance calculations will be limited
  • Precision: We request precise location (not approximate) to accurately show fridge locations

Device Information (if Firebase Analytics is enabled)

  • What we collect: Anonymous device identifier, operating system version, app version, device type
  • Why we collect it: To improve app performance, fix bugs, and understand usage patterns
  • How it's used: Aggregated analytics only - no individual user tracking
  • Linked to user: No - data is anonymous and not linked to your identity
  • Used for tracking: No - we do not use this data for advertising or tracking purposes

Photos (Optional - when you submit a fridge status report)

  • What we collect: Photos you choose to upload when reporting fridge status
  • Why we collect it: To document fridge conditions for the community
  • How it's used: Displayed publicly in fridge profiles for community benefit
  • You control: You choose whether to upload photos - it's completely optional
  • Storage: Photos are stored on our servers (Amazon S3) and are publicly accessible
  • Deletion: Photos become part of the public fridge record and are retained for community reference

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your name, email, or phone number (unless you create an account in future versions)
  • We do NOT sell your data to third parties
  • We do NOT use your location for advertising or tracking
  • We do NOT track your movements outside the app
  • We do NOT collect contact lists, messages, or other personal information

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Map tiles: OpenStreetMap (OSM) provides map tiles. OSM may collect anonymous usage data per their privacy policy: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • API hosting: Your location queries are sent to our API server to fetch nearby fridges. Location data is not stored on our servers.
  • Firebase services: If Firebase Analytics is enabled, Google may collect anonymous usage data per their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • Storage: Location data is not stored. Photos are stored securely on Amazon S3
  • Access: Only authorized personnel can access server logs (which don't contain location data)

Data Retention

  • Location data: Not retained - used only in real-time
  • Device information: Retained in aggregated form for analytics (no individual identifiers)
  • Photos: Retained indefinitely as part of public fridge records

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Disable location access at any time in your device settings or app settings
  • Control photo uploads - you choose whether to upload photos
  • Delete your data: Since we don't store personal data on our servers (except photos you upload), simply uninstalling the app removes all local data
  • Request information: Contact us to learn what data we have about you (if any)

Children's Privacy

FridgeFinder is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

International Users

If you are using FridgeFinder from outside the United States, please note that your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where our servers are located.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Changes will be posted in the app and on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
